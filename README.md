# Three.js Bionic & Horological 3D Lab (`threejs-bionic-mech-lab`)

An AI Agent Skill & Interactive 3D WebGL2 Showcase built on **ES Modules + Three.js v0.186.1 (`r186`) + Physical PBR Shaders** (`iridescence`, `anisotropy`, `sheen`, `clearcoat`).

## Included 3D Interactive Showcases (`examples/`)

1. **`examples/drosophila_connectome_lab_3d.html`** — **FIG. 18: FlyWire 138,639-Neuron Connectome × DeepMind/Janelia `flybody` 59-DoF Lab**
   - Combines the FlyWire v783 adult fruit fly brain connectome (`40ms` gold spike readout solving 9×9 Sudoku across 69/51/36 clues with zero backtracking) and the 59-DoF `flybody` physics model (6-leg tarsal adhesion, non-repeating WCA 3×3 Rubik's Cube scramble & timed restoration, 220Hz vision-guided flight, 721-ommatidia retinotopic vision canvas, and a 120-second 6-Act interactive director showreel).
2. **`examples/mechanical_peacock_3d.html`** — **FIG. 15: Pavo Prismatica · 2D⇄3D Holographic Cloisonné Peacock**
   - 12 subsystems, 2D flat blueprint mandala to 3D sculptural ascension, 19 octahedral prism ocelli eyes, 3-axis gyroscopic armillary sphere core, origami dichroic wings, and 180° train fan blooming.
3. **`examples/mechanical_crocodile_3d.html`** — **FIG. 12: Crocodylus Mechanica · 22.8kN Hydraulic-Jaw Horological Reptile**
   - 4-column pyramidal osteoderm scutes, internal double mainspring & ruby tourbillon gearbox, interlocking 36-tooth hydraulic mandible, and 8-segment sinusoidal sculling tail.
4. **`examples/mechanical_dragonfly_3d.html`** — **FIG. 09: Libellula Mechanica · Quad-Wing Counter-Phase Ornithopter**
   - Coaxial differential gearbox, gold filigree wings with adjustable fore/hind phase lag (`0°–180°`), figure-8 feathering, and 10-segment counter-balanced abdomen.
